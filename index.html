Flagship Solution

NanoRefrigerant

NanoRefrigerant is a one-time compressor lubricant additive designed to improve the efficiency of existing HVAC and refrigeration systems.

The solution works within the existing refrigeration circuit to improve heat transfer and overall system performance without requiring major equipment replacement.
